# Rater instructions (item texts removed)

Task: rate 15 requirement candidates generated by an AI tool from a civil servant's statements about a process; rate texts, not people. Individual work, 60-90 minutes, no consultation of the system, the authors or other raters.

**Task A (Yes/No per criterion; Yes = criterion met):** Unambiguous (one interpretation; fails on undefined terms or vague referents), Verifiable (confirmable by inspection, demonstration, test or analysis, with observable result or threshold), Singular (one need; fails when several actions are joined or several tests are hidden). Rate each criterion separately; when in doubt choose what a strict reviewer would choose and note the reason.

**Task B:** compare the civil servant's statement with the candidate. Fidelity (one of): Faithful, Legitimate expansion, Distortion, Hallucination, Contamination (definitions in `iso29148_rubric.md`). Actor shift: Yes if in the statement a person or unit acts and in the candidate "the system" acts; No otherwise. Actor shift = Yes implies Distortion.

**Baseline task (second iteration):** for each of 20 knowledge items that appeared later in a session, answer Yes / Partial / No to "was it already in the first three responses of the civil servant?", using only those three responses.

Calibration used four fictional items about issuing a certificate (not part of the study data). Do not alter row order or codes.
